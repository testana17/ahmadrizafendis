Common questions

How is the final price decided?

We agree on the scope first: pages or screens, features, and integrations. You get a written quote before any work begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size. A simple website is much quicker than an app with its own backend. I’ll give you a timeline together with the quote.

Can I start small and add features later?

Yes. I build in a way that lets you add pages, features, or an app later without starting over.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I stay available for fixes and improvements after launch. We can agree on the details when we discuss the project.
